Our Storied History

Chapter 1: The "New Fun Slides"

The Republic's story begins on October 15, 2025. Aidyn Kerns created "The New Fun Slides" as a response to other chaotic Google Slideshows. The goal was to create a zone where friends could share content and laugh without fear of their work being deleted or the slides lagging out. Though the initial rules were loose, the slideshow quickly gained popularity, establishing a core group of "higher-ups" including Jack Crawford, Wyatt Belknap, Zach Rose, and Zach Becher.

Chapter 2: The Birth of "Spungbung" & The Republic

A pivotal moment occurred on October 16, 2025, when Wyatt Belknap and Zachary Ludwig created a unique piece of art. Wyatt named this creation "Spungbung," which inspired the core "Spung" ideology and the "Spung Spangled Banner." After a user, Zachary Ludwig, was removed for inappropriate content and then re-added (suspected by Zachary Becher), he deleted all the slides. Aidyn Kerns restored the slides, leading to a "mass removal" and the formal creation of "The Republic of Spung" to establish order. Aidyn Kerns became the first president without an election, and the first Charter of Governance was drafted by Jack Crawford, establishing the positions of Executive Ruler and Executor, won by Jack Crawford and Zachary Rose, respectively.

Chapter 3: The Trial of Zachary Bechar

The first official trial in the Republic's history was against Zachary Bechar, who was charged with treason for re-adding Zachary Ludwig after his removal. He confessed to the charge and, by a unanimous decision, was removed from the slides, setting a precedent for the rule of law.

Chapter 4: The First Chinchillian War

This event, which took place in Ms. Masl's room on November 17, 2025, escalated from a verbal disagreement between Zachary Rose and Zachary Ludwig into a **brief physical altercation**. Following a minor accidental contact, the conflict escalated when Mr. Ludwig used an ***Animal Farm* book in an aggressive manner** against Mr. Rose. The situation involved **minor book throwings and the falling of chairs**, requiring the immediate intervention of others present, including Jackson Clayson, Jack Crawford, and Wyatt Belknap, before Ms. Masl restored order. This intense, yet swiftly resolved, incident highlighted significant political tensions preceding the day's election.

Chapter 5: The First General Election (Nov 17, 2025)

The first scheduled General Election was held on November 17, 2025, marking a peaceful transfer of power and solidifying the Republic's democratic processes. The citizens voted for a new administration and on key issues.

Election Results:
  • Presidential: Wyatt Belknap (Spatian) defeated Landon Boyle (Traditional) with a decisive 12-1 vote.
  • Senator-Executive Ruler: Jack Crawford (Spatian) was elected with a 13-0 vote.
  • Senator-Executioner: Zachary Rose (Traditional) was elected, defeating Aidyn Kerns (Spatian) 11-2.
  • Colony Name: The name "New Spungia" was chosen for the new colony with 5 votes, solidifying the new name of the nation.
  • Issue 1 (Money shares and businesses): Passed with an 8-5 vote, scheduled to be put in place on November 20, 2025.

Political Parties

Includes Spungism values, is "old time like," and uses humor and feelings to get votes. Members include Quintin Carpenter.

Spatian Spung

Includes intelligently-worded Spungism values and pulls values from other civilizations. Members include Jack Crawford, Aidyn Kerns, and Wyatt Belknap.

Citizen Status

Trusted Citizens

Granted exemption from punitive actions by the Executive Authority. They hold no voting rights in government but may serve an advisory role. Status is granted by a joint majority vote of the President and Senate.

Designated Citizens

Exempt from actions by the Executor. However, they are formally disenfranchised and barred from all electoral and legislative processes. Status is granted by the President and Senatorial Council.

Charter of Governance

Part I: Principles of Conduct and Digital Integrity

Chapter 1: Code of Conduct for Digital Spaces
  • Section 1.1.1: Any act of deletion, alteration, or unauthorized modification of official rule sets, governmental statutes, or presentation slides is strictly prohibited.
  • Section 1.1.2: Presentation slides designated for "4simpleproblems (4SP)" shall not be deleted or altered without authorization.
  • Section 1.1.3: A maximum limit of five (5) animated GIFs is imposed per slide. Excessive propagation of GIFs is classified as digital spam.
  • Section 1.2.2: Designated user slides are inviolable and shall not be subject to editing, modification, or deletion by any other party.
  • Section 1.4.1 (First Infraction): A three (3) day suspension of all access and editing privileges.
  • Section 1.4.2 (Subsequent Infractions): A third (3rd) or subsequent infraction shall result in permanent and irrevocable revocation of all access rights.

Part II: The Framework of Republican Governance

Chapter 2 & 3: Senate, Executive Authorities
  • Section 2.1.1: The Senate comprises two principal leadership positions: the Executive Ruler and the Executor.
  • Section 2.2.2: In the event of a tie vote between the Executive Ruler and Executor on Major Legislation, the President shall cast the deciding vote.
  • Section 2.2.3 (Presidential Veto): The President retains the absolute right to veto any legislative act. This veto is final and not subject to override. (Note: Section 4.2.2 limits this to 3 vetoes per term).
  • Section 2.3.1: Senators shall be elected to serve a term of one (1) calendar month.
  • Section 4.1.1: The President shall hold office for a fixed term of one (1) consecutive month.

Part III: Status, Rights, and Judicial Frameworks

Chapter 5 & 6: Articles of Trust
  • Section 5.1.1: Trusted Citizens are exempt from disciplinary actions by the Executive Authority.
  • Section 5.1.2: Trusted Citizens shall not possess any voting rights within the legislative or executive processes.
  • Section 5.1.4: Approval of Trusted Citizen status requires a joint deliberation and majority vote from the President and the Senate.
  • Section 6.1.1: Designated Citizens are exempt from any punitive actions or influence from the Executor.
  • Section 6.2.1: Designated Citizens are formally disenfranchised and barred from all electoral and legislative processes.

Part IV: Electoral Frameworks and Legislative Enactment

Chapter 7: Bylaws Governing Elections
  • Section 7.1.3: No legislative voting shall be permitted on any day officially designated for a scheduled election.
  • Section 7.2.1 (Minor Laws): The sole authority for enactment of Minor Laws rests with the Executive Ruler.
  • Section 7.2.2 (Major Laws): Must first be submitted for approval to both members of the Senatorial Council. If they cannot reach consensus, it is submitted to the President. If the President is undecided, it is put to a popular referendum.
  • Section 7.2.3 (Vascular Laws): Any set of four (4) or more laws presented concurrently. Requires approval from at least two (2) of the three (3) officials (President, Executive Ruler, Executor).
  • Section 7.2.4 (Removing Laws): No Power of Government can remove a law. This requires a 6-step voting process involving a citizen, the President, the Senators (100% agreement), Trusted Citizens (90% agreement), and Regular Citizens (75% agreement).

Part X: The Establishment of Colonies

Chapter 13: Establishment of Colonies
  • Article 13.1: Establishment of an official colony requires an official Republic of Spung Charter (RSC). The RSC must secure affirmative approval from a minimum of two (2) designated Council members.
  • Article 13.7: The designation and naming of a colony shall be the prerogative of the Council, following the submission of a suggestion by the Governor.
